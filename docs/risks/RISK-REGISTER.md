## Notes

- **Scoring**: Probability (1–4) × Impact (1–4). Score 1–3 = Low, 4–6 = Medium, 7–9 = High, 10–16 = Critical.
- **Owner abbreviations** per `docs/parity/README.md`: App Lead, Infra Lead, WinForms Lead, Reports Lead, Localization Owner, Security Owner, Ops Owner, QA Lead, Product Owner.
- **Evidence links** should point to the actual artifact (markdown file, test result, CI log, reconciliation report, approval record).
- **Status values**: Open, In Progress, Mitigated, Accepted, Transferred, Blocking.
- **Incident templates** referenced: `docs/risks/R3-migration-incident.md`, `docs/risks/R4-transaction-incident.md`, `docs/risks/R11-recovery-incident.md`.

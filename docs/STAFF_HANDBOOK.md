# BREWFLOW POS — Staff Handbook

## Overview

Welcome to the BREWFLOW POS Staff Handbook. This guide covers the day-to-day procedures all staff should follow when working shifts, handling cash, closing registers, allocating tips, and operating the loyalty program.

---

## 1. Shift Procedures

### Opening Your Shift

1. Arrive at your assigned register.
2. Navigate to **Shifts** and open your shift with your opening float amount.
3. Verify the opening float matches the physical cash in your drawer.
4. Confirm your shift status shows as `open`.

### During Your Shift

- Process orders through the **Orders** page.
- Record any cash in (adding change) or cash out (safe drops) using the Cash In / Cash Out buttons.
- Keep your cash drawer balanced throughout the shift.

### Ending Your Shift

1. Count the cash in your drawer.
2. Record any final cash in or cash out.
3. Click **Request Shift Close**.
4. Wait for a Branch Manager to approve the close.

### Shift Handover

If you need to leave before your shift ends:

1. Click **Hand Over Shift** while your shift is open.
2. Select a qualified colleague to take over.
3. Review the cash float summary with your colleague.
4. Click **Hand Over Shift** to transfer responsibility.

Your colleague becomes the new shift holder and is responsible for closing the shift.

---

## 2. Manager Approval for Shift Close

Branch Managers review and approve shift closures:

1. When a staff member requests shift close, the shift enters `pending_approval` state.
2. The Branch Manager opens **Shifts** and clicks **Review Manager Approval**.
3. The Manager reviews:
   - Cash in / cash out / net total
   - Any tips allocated
4. The Manager enters approval notes and clicks **Approve & Close** or **Reject**.
5. If rejected, the shift remains open and the staff member should address the issue and re-request close.

---

## 3. Tips Allocation

After a shift is closed and approved by a Branch Manager, tips can be distributed:

1. Navigate to **Shifts** and find your closed shift.
2. Click **Allocate Tips**.
3. Enter the total tip amount collected during the shift.
4. Add recipients:
   - Select each staff member from the dropdown.
   - Enter the tip amount for each person.
5. Verify the allocated amounts sum exactly to the total tip amount.
6. Click **Allocate Tips**.

**Rule:** All allocated amounts must equal the total tip amount exactly. The system will not allow allocation if the amounts don't match.

## 4. Loyalty Program — Staff Procedures

### Customer Enrollment

When a new customer wants to join the loyalty program:

1. Navigate to **Loyalty**.
2. Collect the customer's full name (required), and optionally email and phone.
3. Click **Enroll Customer**.
4. Inform the customer they start with 0 points at Standard tier.

### Earning Points

When a customer makes a qualifying purchase:

1. Look up the customer in **Loyalty**.
2. Enter points earned based on your store's loyalty rules.
3. Enter a reason (e.g., purchase description).
4. Click **Award Points**.

### Redeeming Points

When a customer wants to redeem points:

1. Verify the customer has sufficient points balance.
2. Enter the points to redeem.
3. Enter a reason (e.g., reward item).
4. Click **Redeem Points**.
5. Confirm the redemption with the customer.

### Customer Disenrollment

If a customer requests to leave the loyalty program:

1. Find the customer in **Loyalty**.
2. Click **Disenroll**.
3. Confirm permanent deletion.

**Warning:** Disenrollment removes all loyalty data permanently and cannot be reversed.

## 5. Closing Procedures Checklist

Before leaving at the end of your shift:

- [ ] All orders processed and recorded
- [ ] All cash in / cash out recorded
- [ ] Drawer counted and balanced
- [ ] Shift close requested
- [ ] Branch Manager approval obtained
- [ ] Tips allocated (if applicable)
- [ ] Shift finalized and closed
- [ ] Register logged out

---

## 6. Code of Conduct

- Never share your login credentials with anyone.
- Log out when stepping away from the register.
- Report any cash discrepancies to your Branch Manager immediately.
- Handle customer data respectfully and in accordance with privacy policies.
- Follow all tips allocation rules — do not allocate tips to yourself beyond your entitled share.
